# Sound credits

Every `.wav` file here is generated by `tools/make-sounds.py` from oscillators,
filtered noise and a plucked-string model. They contain no recordings or
third-party samples.

They are dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): copy, modify and
use them for anything, without asking.
